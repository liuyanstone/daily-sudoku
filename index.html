    YOUR LITTLE FIELD GUIDE

    One of each. Everywhere.

    Fill the board so that each row, each column, and each 3×3 box contains 1–9 exactly once. The printed numbers are fixed.

    Select a square, then use the number buttons or your keyboard. Turn on Notes to pencil possible numbers into an empty square. Undo and Erase help you change your mind.

    Repeated numbers are highlighted. Check reports conflicts; it does not reveal answers. A completed, valid board earns the day’s difficulty points once.

    There is one puzzle per local calendar day, with exactly one solution. Difficulty varies by day. Easy earns 300 points, Medium 600, Hard 1,000, and Expert 1,500. No time limit or speed penalty.

    Your entries, notes, and score are saved on this browser/device. Reopening resumes today’s puzzle. Come back tomorrow for a new one.
